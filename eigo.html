解答と解説

1 1 studying　2 running　3 swimming　4 writing　5 using　6 coming　7 sitting　8 playing　9 getting　10 dying（eで終わる語はeをとる／短母音＋子音字は子音字を重ねる／ieはyに変える）

2 (1) Close the door.　(2) Please speak slowly.　(3) Be quiet in the library.　(4) Don't take pictures here.（Please don't take pictures here.）　(5) Let's not play tennis.　(6) Let's have lunch together.

3 (1) I cannot swim.（I can't swim.）　(2) Can you swim? — Yes, I can.／No, I cannot.（can't）　(3) She can speak English.　(4) I cannot play the piano.　(5) Can you sing well?　(6) Can I take pictures here?　(7) 動詞の原形。canは「〜できる」という意味を加える助動詞で、時制や人称の変化はcan自身が担うため、後ろの動詞は形を変えないから。　(8) 彼は英語を上手に話すことができる。

4 (1) He is running.　(2) They are playing soccer.　(3) Are you watching TV?　(4) It is raining now.　(5) What is your mother doing?　(6) She is not reading a book.（She isn't reading a book.）　(7) She is doing her homework.　(8) 「今、〜している最中である」という、進行中の動作を表す。

5 (1) Go straight.　(2) Turn right at the second corner.　(3) The library is on your left.　(4) Where is the post office?　(5) It is in front of the station.　(6) Turn left at the intersection.

6 1 take care of　2 arrive at/in　3 visit　4 trip（journey）　5 famous　6 between　7 prepare（get ready）　8 look for
